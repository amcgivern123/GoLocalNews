# Newry Electric Gate Repairs - 12 months guarantee on all gate automation repairs

*Local gate automation specialist AutoGate NI introduces a full 12-month guarantee across all residential and commercial electric gate repairs in Newry and across Northern Ireland.*

**NEWRY, NORTHERN IRELAND – 22 November 2026** – AutoGate NI, a family-run electric gate installation and repair specialist based in Newry, has announced a comprehensive **12-month guarantee on all electric gate and gate automation repairs**. The commitment is designed to give homeowners, landlords, and commercial property managers across Newry, South Down, South Armagh, and beyond complete confidence in the longevity and safety of their automated entrances.

When an electric gate fails—whether stuck closed before the morning commute or left standing open overnight—property owners face both immediate inconvenience and a serious security risk. AutoGate NI’s 12-month repair guarantee directly addresses a common frustration in the industry: quick, temporary fixes that break down again within weeks. Every repair carried out by the company focuses on diagnosing the root mechanical or electrical fault, providing an upfront price before work begins, and testing all safety devices before signing off the job.

> “A gate that doesn’t work is a security liability, and a quick patch job isn’t good enough when a family’s safety or a business’s perimeter is on the line,” said **Adrian McGivern, founder of AutoGate NI**. “With 25 years of hands-on experience in gate automation, we know how to find the real cause of a fault and fix it properly the first time. Backing every single repair with a full 12-month guarantee is our way of standing squarely behind our workmanship.”

### Comprehensive Repairs for All Major Gate Automation Brands

AutoGate NI repairs swing, sliding, underground, overground, and hydraulic gate systems from all leading manufacturers—regardless of who originally installed them. Supported brands include **Beninca, CAME, FAAC, BFT, Nice, SEA, Gibidi, Allmatic, V2, Key Automation, Chamberlain LiftMaster, DEA System, and Fadini**.

Common faults covered under AutoGate NI’s diagnostic and repair service include:

- **Motors, gearboxes, and control boards:** Resolving worn components, hydraulic leaks, water ingress, and storm or power-cut damage.
- **Safety sensors and compliance:** Repairing or replacing photocells and safety edges, with every repaired gate checked for safe operation and force-tested to **BS EN 12453** standards.
- **Access control:** Troubleshooting and replacing unresponsive remote fobs, keypads, and audio/video intercom systems.
- **Mechanical and structural issues:** Fixing sagging or misaligned gates, worn hinges, and obstructed or damaged sliding gate tracks.
